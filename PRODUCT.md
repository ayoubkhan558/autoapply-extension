# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers completing application forms across ATS platforms. They need accurate, fast autofill while retaining review and control before submission.

## Product Purpose

AutoApply stores reusable candidate profiles locally and fills job-application forms reliably, including repeated education and experience sections. Success means less repetitive typing without silently submitting or inventing candidate information.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, trustworthy, efficient.

## Anti-references

Generic AI dashboards, decorative gradients, glass effects, excessive cards, dark-only tools, and interfaces that obscure what data will be filled.

## Design Principles

- Keep the candidate in control; fill and explain, never submit.
- Prefer familiar controls and visible system status.
- Make dense profile data easy to scan and edit.
- Preserve privacy by keeping profile data local unless the user explicitly invokes an AI feature.
- Optimize for accuracy before clever automation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Light theme only. Target WCAG 2.1 AA, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
